# A closed issue never counts as new analysis

## Choice

Closing an issue takes it out of every person's new analysis. A closed
issue, and every answer or revision on it, never counts as new, and
shows only among the closed issues, where a person still reads its
last answers.

## Rationale

New analysis is the inbox a person works: the issues that may still
need a ruling, a comment, or a first read before the next planning
session. A closed issue needs none of those. Its close names its
reason, and a person who wants to veto a close finds it among the
closed issues. Keeping closed issues out keeps the inbox to what a
person can still act on.

## Alternatives

- An answer on a closed issue counts as new until each person reads it — closed issues fill the inbox, and each one must be opened to clear it.
- Closing an issue marks its answers read in every person's ledger — the close writes into ledgers git ignores, so every other clone's inbox keeps the closed issue.
