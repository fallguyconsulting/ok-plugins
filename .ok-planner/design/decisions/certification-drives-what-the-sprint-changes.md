# Under a suite, sprint certification drives the stories its sprint changes

## Choice

Where the project's testing is on or frozen, sprint certification
drives the sprint's new and changed stories alone: each story a delta
of the sprint adds or amends, and each story a work item of the sprint
names among those it realizes. A sprint that names no such story runs
no drive. Where testing is off, certification drives every story. The
whole-product drive runs on the owner's cadence, apart from any sprint
(see also: one-drive-procedure-fixes-nothing,
drive-tries-each-story-as-a-user).

## Rationale

The drive is where a run spends its tokens, and its cost follows the
number of stories it takes, while in certification its yield follows
how new the stories are. On one project, a certification that drove
all 113 stories spent more of the sprint's cost on the drive than on
anything else and found no defect in the sprint's change, while the
project's suite covered 111 of those stories. On another, whose sprint
added all 16 of its stories, the drive cost about 2% of execution and
found two defects in the sprint's own change that no planned test
named. Where a suite runs beside it, certification drives the stories
whose promise or delivery the sprint changes, where a driver finds
what the plan did not name, and the whole-product drive finds the
outcomes no scenario names across older code. A story the sprint's
deltas add or amend carries a promise the sprint changes, and a story
a work item realizes carries a delivery it changes; both are named in
the sprint the owner approved, so the drive's reach follows the
sprint, not how widely the code cites stories. Where testing is off,
no test checks a story the sprint does not name, so certification
drives every story, and a sprint's break in older code reaches the
intake at the close that meets it. The cost: under a suite, a story a
sprint breaks without naming it stays undriven until the owner's next
whole-product drive, unless a test catches it first, and with testing
off, every certification pays for a whole-product drive.

## Alternatives

- Certification drives every story under every testing state — a
  sprint's break in a story it does not name surfaces at its own
  close, and every certification pays for a whole-product drive that,
  beside a suite, finds little in the change.
- Certification narrows its drive under every testing state — a
  cheaper close with testing off, and a break in a story the sprint
  does not name then meets no check before the owner's next
  whole-product drive.
- Certification drives only the stories its deltas add or amend —
  cheaper still, and a story whose delivery a work item rebuilds goes
  undriven at the close that changed it.
- No drive in certification where the project's suite runs — the
  cheapest close, and a defect in the sprint's own change that no
  planned test names ships.
- Where a sprint names no story, certification drives one story the
  changed files cite — every certification holds a driver, and that
  driver is chosen by annotation, not by what the sprint changes.
- Certification drives the sprint's stories and every story whose
  citing code the change touched — its reach follows how widely the
  changed code cites stories, and ends where the annotations end.
- A full drive gates each planning session — the same cost per sprint,
  and a sprint's own regression surfaces only after the sprint closed.
