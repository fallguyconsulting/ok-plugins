# Capture an idea without committing to it

## Story

As a project owner, I want to capture a speculative idea in one pass, without triggering planning or authorizing implementation, so that thinking is externalized and revisitable at no ceremony cost.

## Acceptance

The owner names a topic → a single dated sketch document lands among the project's live records containing the idea, its shape, open questions, and risks, stamped as not a sprint and not authorization to build; the durable corpus and the intake queue are untouched; the session ends at the sketch without chaining into planning. The sketch skill is the real producing component.

## Falsifier

Capturing an idea mutates the corpus or the queue; the sketch silently upgrades into planning or implementation; or the capture demands dialogue and review before the idea can land.

## Proof

Example — a sketch produced from a one-line topic that a third party can read as a thinking record, verifiably absent from the durable corpus, the queue, and any authorization path.
