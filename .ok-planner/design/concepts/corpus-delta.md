# Corpus delta

## What it is

A corpus delta is one unit of change to the design corpus, to the project's coding standards, to a docspec, or to the surface intent, carried inside a sprint: one artifact, a concept, story, decision, subject, practice, or docspec, or the surface intent, made new, amended, or retired. Applying a delta is how that content changes.

## Purpose

Deltas make corpus change reviewable at planning and checkable after execution: the owner signs off on the change itself during planning, and the corpus after execution can be compared with what the owner approved.

## Boundaries

A delta changes exactly one artifact. Deltas exist only inside sprints (see also: sprint); what they change is the design corpus, the project's coding standards, its docspecs, or its surface intent (see also: design-corpus, subject, practice, docspec, surface-intent). The form a delta takes, and how it is verified and landed, are decisions (see also: final-form-deltas under decisions; completion-contract).
