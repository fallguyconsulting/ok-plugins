# Sprint

## What it is

A sprint is the planning ceremony's terminal artifact: a change-order
against the design corpus, the project's coding standards, its
docspecs, and its surface intent, expressed as corpus deltas plus the
flat, unordered work items that realize them, with any defect issues
it carries, terminated by a fixed completion contract. It is a sprint in the scrum sense — a collection
of potentially disparate changes with no required unifying focus and
no manufactured narrative.

## Purpose

The sprint is the whole interface between planning and execution.
Because it is self-sufficient — everything the work needs, in final
form — any executor works from the same brief. Staging and sequencing belong to execution
time (see also: build-task-per-stage under decisions).

## Boundaries

A sprint owns approved intent: deltas, work items, any defect issues it carries, and the completion contract. What a work item may be is a decision (see also: work-items-change-the-product under decisions;
issue). What a sprint does with defect issues is a decision (see also: taken-defects-fixed-by-certification under
decisions; defect). What a sprint does about tests is a decision (see also:
testing-runs-inside-the-sprint, test-tasks-own-the-tests,
planned-tests-name-their-waits under decisions). A documentation
sprint is not a sprint in this sense (see also: docspec;
documentation-sprints-write-the-documentation under decisions). How a sprint is executed is a decision (see also: build-task-per-stage, team-execution-cold-gate, and task-tools-mirror-the-report under
decisions). A sprint does NOT own execution order, which belongs to execution
(see also: build-task-per-stage under decisions), and it is NOT
the intake queue (see also: issue, corpus-delta,
completion-contract, plan-a-sprint under stories). The record of its
execution is its companion completion report (see also:
completion-report). Sprints are project records under the estate's
record discipline (see also: estate, design-corpus;
records-stay-out-of-context and closing-commit-baseline under
decisions).
