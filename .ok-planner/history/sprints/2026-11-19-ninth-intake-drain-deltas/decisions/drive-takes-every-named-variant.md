# A driver drives every variant the story's text names

## Choice

On each surface a story is offered through, its driver drives every
variant the story's own text names, such as each way a story says its
results can be narrowed, and only those. It judges each variant on its
own, as it judges each surface. The order it acts in is a decision of
its own (see also: drive-acts-in-a-first-time-users-order). It reports
each variant it drove, so the merge sees what a pass
covers (see also: drive-tries-each-story-as-a-user).

## Rationale

The story bounds a drive because the story is the promise
certification judges: driving each variant its text names widens the
drive exactly as far as the owner's own words go, and needs nothing
written down. Judging each variant on its own keeps a pass on one way
from hiding a failure on another.

## Alternatives

- A driver stops at the first path that delivers the benefit —
  cheapest, and a pass can hide a failing variant the story names.
- A driver lists every path on each surface, each flag, control, and
  order of actions, and drives each — bounded only by the product's
  size, and the merge must judge a list's completeness with nothing to
  judge it against.
