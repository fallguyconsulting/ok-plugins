# Any person who works the intake may comment and rule, and the latest ruling stands

## Choice

Any person who works the intake may comment and rule; the latest ruling stands.

## Rationale

Letting anyone rule, with the latest ruling standing, keeps one
person's absence from holding the intake.

## Alternatives

- Only one named person may rule — every ruling waits on that person.
